# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the system design direction and ensure the solution is technically sound, scalable, and aligned with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define solution architecture and technical standards
- Evaluate trade-offs between approaches, platforms, and dependencies
- Review designs for scalability, security, and maintainability
- Guide technical decision-making with project and engineering leaders
- Identify architectural risks and mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Deliver a solution that is sustainable and scalable
- Reduce rework caused by poor technical decisions
- Align implementation with the long-term platform strategy

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical risk assessments
- Guidance to developers, project managers, and stakeholders on technical trade-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation choices and technical feasibility
- Aligns with Product Managers on trade-offs related to scope, quality, and delivery constraints
- Supports Project Managers by highlighting technical risks, dependencies, and delivery impacts
- Collaborates with Release Managers to ensure deployment readiness and operational considerations

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads establish quality standards, testing procedures, and defect management practices to ensure the work meets business and technical expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality gates and acceptance criteria
- Manage testing strategy across functional, integration, and regression coverage
- Track defects, priorities, and resolution progress
- Coordinate with engineering on test automation and quality improvements
- Report quality status and delivery risks to project leadership

### Goals
- Reduce defects and release risk
- Improve product confidence and reliability
- Ensure quality is built into delivery rather than added at the end

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and defect review meetings
- Quality status reports to Project Managers and stakeholders
- Coordination with Developers and Release Managers on sign-off criteria

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers on automated testing and defect triage
- Supports Project Managers with quality risks and readiness assessments
- Coordinates with Release Managers to confirm release quality gates are met
- Provides insights to Product Managers on user-facing quality impacts and trade-offs

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the planning, communication, and execution of software releases to reduce risk and ensure smooth delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Plan release windows, sequencing, and deployment checklists
- Coordinate with teams on release readiness and rollback planning
- Communicate release updates and expected impacts to stakeholders
- Ensure deployment procedures are documented and followed
- Monitor post-release health and escalate issues as needed

### Goals
- Deliver stable, predictable releases
- Minimize customer impact and operational disruption
- Improve release readiness and recovery planning

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews
- Deployment checklists and change notices
- Post-release review and incident follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to confirm release readiness
- Supports Project Managers on milestone and deployment timing
- Collaborates with Stakeholder Liaison to communicate release impact and schedule
- Coordinates with Technical Architects on operational readiness and rollback considerations

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons ensure effective communication between the project team and external stakeholders, helping maintain alignment and manage expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Act as a primary communication channel with stakeholders
- Gather stakeholder feedback and summarize priorities
- Ensure stakeholders receive timely and relevant updates
- Escalate concerns that need decision-making or intervention
- Facilitate reviews, approvals, and alignment checkpoints

### Goals
- Maintain trust and transparency with stakeholders
- Align stakeholder expectations with delivery realities
- Improve decision speed and reduce confusion

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and status updates
- Feedback summaries and prioritization inputs
- Review meetings and approval workflows

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers in stakeholder engagement strategies
- Coordinates with Product Managers on feedback and requirement alignment
- Shares release and milestone information with Release Managers
- Keeps executive sponsors informed of status, risks, and blockers

---

## Process Owner

### Role Summary
Process Owners maintain the quality, consistency, and continual improvement of project management and delivery processes.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and update project process documentation
- Identify process gaps and improvement opportunities
- Ensure standard methods are applied consistently across teams
- Support adoption of best practices and lessons learned
- Drive process improvement initiatives and retrospectives

### Goals
- Increase process consistency and clarity
- Improve execution quality and team efficiency
- Support sustainable organizational learning

### Typical Communication
- Process reviews and governance updates
- Retrospective findings and improvement recommendations
- Cross-team coordination on process adoption

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to align processes with project execution needs
- Supports Product Managers in prioritization and delivery clarity
- Coordinates with QA and Release teams on quality and deployment standards
- Collaborates with Technical Architects on governance and technical process controls

---
